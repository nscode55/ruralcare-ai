# 🩺 RuralCare AI – Smart Symptom Checker

An AI-powered symptom checker designed for rural and underserved communities to assess possible health conditions, understand severity, and receive guidance on when to seek medical care.

---

## 🌍 Problem
Access to basic healthcare is limited in many rural areas. People often delay seeking medical help due to lack of awareness, availability, or affordability, which can lead to serious health risks.

---

## 💡 Solution
RuralCare AI helps users:
- Input symptoms easily  
- Get predicted health conditions  
- Understand severity  
- Receive simple medical guidance  

This supports early diagnosis and improves healthcare accessibility.

---

## ⚙️ Features
- Symptom-based disease prediction  
- Simple and user-friendly interface  
- Health recommendations  
- Fast and lightweight application  

---

## 🧠 Tech Stack
- Python  
- Pandas  
- NumPy  
- Scikit-learn  
- Streamlit
